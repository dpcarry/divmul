# Detailed Fixed-Level Shared Mantissa Circuits

Independent schematic drafts for the current production shared fixed L0-L3
DIV/MUL RTL. No manuscript, existing figures, canonical CSVs, or RTL changed.
These are RTL-operation schematics, not mapped-cell placement/netlist drawings.
The plus symbol denotes the multioperand sum, not a claimed single physical adder.
The exact multiplier symbols retain the current `*` implementation; no new
handwritten shift/add coefficient implementation is assumed.

Source: `rtl/root_opt/oadm_fixed_divmul_root_opt.v`; hash in `source.json`.
Reproduce: `.venv/bin/python experiments/arithmetic_sharing/circuit_figures/draw.py`.
Each fixed level has PDF, editable SVG, and PNG exports.

`oadm_fixed_l0_circuit.drawio` is the independently editable diagrams.net
source for the detailed L0 schematic. Its blocks and connections follow the
same current RTL configuration; it is not generated by `draw.py`.

- x_m/y_m are 24-bit normalized mantissa codes, not complete FP32 words.
- Named K_x/K_y and P_x/P_y terminals refer to the same nets across sections.
- K_x=16*k_x and K_y=16*k_y; midpoint selection is exact.
- m is the live operation input: 1 DIV, 0 MUL. Level is fixed at elaboration.
- Gray rectangles represent recoding, bit slices, fixed shifts, or arithmetic.
  They need not survive as distinct cells after synthesis.
- The selected residual is signed. Its width is sized for MUL; the DIV branch
  zero-pads low bits to preserve the same binary scaling, not sign extension.
- W is the 29-bit integer plane_value representation of the local plane.
  The indicated sign extensions, fixed-width arithmetic and shifts follow RTL.
- The DIV coefficient multiplication is unsigned on W[24:drop] and C, with
  a full retained-operand product and zero-extension to the 29-bit output.
- MUL bypasses the w_n slice and coefficient multiplication.
- V is core_value, before normalization and the common normal-finite FP32
  sign/exponent/packing shell. Those peripheral circuits are outside this figure.
- These are fixed-level calibrated-coefficient/balanced-MUL-bias configurations,
  NOT the runtime 10/14 datapath or the input-masking/narrowing experiment.

| Level | Natural signed residual bits | DIV drop | MUL drop | Selected width | DIV padded zeros | W drop | Coefficient bits |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| L0 | 23 | 18 | 16 | 7 | 2 | 18 | 7 |
| L1 | 22 | 16 | 14 | 8 | 2 | 16 | 7 |
| L2 | 21 | 16 | 12 | 9 | 4 | 16 | 8 |
| L3 | 20 | 16 | 10 | 10 | 6 | 16 | 8 |

For example, L3 encodes r_x={~x_m[19],x_m[18:0]}, selects either
{r_x[19:16],6'b0} or r_x[19:10], and computes P_x=r_x_selected*K_y.
The 29-bit sign-extended product shifts left by 6. The midpoint term shifts
left by 15, and the MUL compensation (K_x+K_y) shifts left by 5. DIV takes
W[24:16]*C and shifts the result left by 8. All match the existing RTL.
