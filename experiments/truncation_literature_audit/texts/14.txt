   The paper proposes a design that adopts logarithmic divider [7],
and minimizes the approximations by employing exponent expan-
sion series. The proposed divider was compared with logarithmic

expansion to establish multiplication of the inverse of divisor with
dividend. A Newton Raphson method to establish inverse of divisor
and then multiplying with dividend was also proposed in [10]. The
proposed divider is utilized for color quantization scheme which
is widely used in image and video processing applications. Color
quantization is a regular method employed to compress the im-
age preserving overall appearance and information. K-mean color
quantization technique is one of the quantization algorithm which
uses mean update step in each of the iterations, thereby an efficient
